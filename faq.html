Styles and design

Will switching style delete my content? No. A style changes fonts, colors, shapes and spacing. Your products, pages, images, text and section order stay as they are.

Can I use my own fonts? Elevra uses fonts from Shopify's font library, which is large and fast. Uploading your own font needs a code change; a Shopify Partner can help.

Why does my text look different over a photo? Sections over photos (slideshows, promo cards) use color scheme 3, which is dark with light text. Change it in Theme settings → Colors, and adjust the overlay setting on the slide or card if the photo is very light.

How do I keep the important part of a photo visible? Set a focal point: click the image in the editor, then Edit and choose the focal point. Elevra keeps it in view when the photo is cropped.

Products and collections

How do I show a "Sale" badge? Give the product a Compare-at price higher than its price. The badge appears automatically. Choose "Sale" or a percentage in Theme settings → Product cards → Sale badge.

When does "Selling fast" appear? Only when Shopify tracks the product's inventory and the quantity left is at or below the number in Theme settings → Product cards. It never appears for products without tracked inventory, so it's always true.

When does "New" appear? For products added within the number of days you choose in Theme settings → Product cards. Set it to 0 to turn it off.

How do I show color swatches? In the product, set swatches on the option values (Shopify admin → product → variant option → swatch). Then turn on Theme settings → Product cards → Show color swatches.

Can sold-out products appear last? Yes: Collection products → Show sold-out products last. It reorders products within each page of results.

How do I set up filters? Install Shopify's Search & Discovery app and choose filters there (availability, price, type, vendor, options and more). Elevra shows them as a sidebar, a drawer or a bar above the products (Collection products → Desktop filter layout).

Cart and checkout

How do I offer gift wrapping? Create a product called, for example, "Gift wrapping" with a price, then choose it in Theme settings → Cart → Gift wrap product. Customers add it with a checkbox in the cart.

Where do the delivery dates come from? From Theme settings → Shipping and delivery: processing time, fastest and slowest delivery, cut-off hour and weekend shipping. Set them to match your real shipping.

Can I show a countdown timer for sales? Yes, with the Countdown section, to a real end date you choose. It never restarts or fakes urgency.

Languages and countries

Which languages does Elevra support? English, French, German, Spanish, Arabic, Hebrew and Urdu are included. Arabic, Hebrew and Urdu display right to left automatically. Add a language in Settings → Languages, and edit any wording in Online Store → Themes → ⋯ → Edit default theme content.

How do customers change country or currency? Turn on Show country/region selector in the Header or Footer. Countries and currencies come from Settings → Markets.

Accessibility and speed

Is Elevra accessible? Elevra is built to WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard navigation everywhere, screen reader labels, readable contrast in every style, and no motion for customers who turn on "reduce motion". Keep it that way by writing image descriptions (alt text) for your images and keeping text readable on your colors.

How do I keep my store fast? Use images around 1600 to 2400 pixels wide (Shopify resizes them for each screen), keep the number of apps low, and avoid very long home pages with many videos.

Updates and support

How do I update the theme? When an update is available, Shopify shows it in Online Store → Themes. Updating creates a new copy with your settings and content; check it, then publish it. Code edits you made aren't carried over, so note them first.

Where do I get help? See the help center.
