Designing your footer

On this page

  • 1. Pick a layout
  • 2. Add your brand
  • 3. Add blocks
  • 4. Set up the bottom bar
  • 5. Optional extras
  • Accessibility
  • How the demo stores do it

The footer sits at the bottom of every page. In the editor, scroll the left sidebar to Footer and click it. Settings for the whole footer are on the right; the parts inside it (menus, newsletter, contact details and so on) are blocks you add, reorder and remove on the left.

Nothing changes when you update from an earlier Elevra version: every new option starts switched off or set to the look you had.

1. Pick a layout

Layout sets how the blocks are arranged. Every layout works on phones, and in right-to-left languages such as Arabic and Hebrew.

LayoutLooks likeGood forExample
EditorialA large brand statement above the columnsMost stores; the defaultDesktop, mobile
ColumnsEven columns of menus and blocksStores with many linksDesktop, mobile
CenteredA widely spaced store name, menus as centered rows of small capitalsSmall catalogs, a calm lookDesktop, mobile
GridColumns in bordered cells that share fine dividersStructured, technical brandsDesktop, mobile
SplitA photo with your brand statement over it on one side, blocks on the otherLifestyle brands with strong photographyDesktop, mobile
MonogramYour store name between fine rules, centered columns with dividersFashion and luxuryDesktop, mobile
RunwayYour store name in a band that slides sideways as customers scroll, the newsletter leadingBold, fashion-led brandsDesktop, mobile
MinimalOne line: your name, menus as a row of links, social iconsStores that want the footer out of the wayDesktop, mobile

Then fine-tune:

  • Width: Page lines the footer up with your pages; Full runs it edge to edge.
  • Columns on desktop: Auto fits the blocks you have; or pick 2 to 6.
  • Menus on mobile: Accordions folds each menu under its heading on phones so the footer stays short; Stacked shows every link.
  • Top spacing, Bottom spacing and Show a line above the footer.
  • Color scheme: one of the six schemes from Theme settings → Colors.

2. Add your brand

  • Brand statement: a short line set large at the top, such as your promise. Leave it empty to hide it.
  • Show store name with Store name position: Top adds a wordmark to the Centered, Split and Monogram layouts; Bottom sets your name large across the full width at the very foot of any layout.
  • Image: the photo for the Split layout.
  • Add a Logo block for your logo (or store name) with a short tagline.

3. Add blocks

Click Add block inside the footer. You can have up to 12. A block only shows when it has something real in it, so an empty block never leaves a gap.

BlockWhat it shows
MenuA menu from Online Store → Navigation. Turn off Collapse on mobile to keep this one open on phones.
TextA heading and a paragraph, such as "About us".
NewsletterAn email signup with four field styles. Add an Incentive only if you really give it. Subscribers appear in Customers.
LogoYour logo or store name, with a tagline.
ContactEmail, phone, WhatsApp, address and opening hours; each becomes a tap-to-contact link. Leave out anything you don't offer.
Social mediaYour profiles from Theme settings → Social media, as icons, names, or both.
Store informationYour shops' addresses and hours, from the same entries as the store locator (Content → Metaobjects → Store location).
ImageA photo with an optional caption and link.
CollectionsCollections you pick, as small image cards or a list.
App badgesApp Store and Google Play badges. Upload the official badge artwork; a badge shows only with both its link and its image.
PromisesUp to four promises with icons, such as free returns, shown in a row across the top of the footer. Only promise what you do.
Custom LiquidCode from an app or a developer.

Apps can add their own blocks too.

4. Set up the bottom bar

The thin bar at the very bottom holds the legal and practical details.

  • Use a separate color scheme to set it apart from the main footer.
  • Arrangement on desktop: one Row, or Stacked lines.
  • Copyright text: write [year] for the current year, for example "© [year] Maison Rose". Empty shows the year and your store name.
  • Show payment icons in Monochrome (matches your colors) or Color. The icons come from your enabled payment methods.
  • Show policy links: from Settings → Policies. Fill in your refund, privacy, terms and shipping policies first.
  • Show "Powered by Shopify".
  • Country/region and language selectors appear when you sell in more than one market or language (Settings → Markets and Settings → Languages). They open as a small panel; customers can close it with Escape.

5. Optional extras

  • Show social media links and Show Follow on Shop (needs Shop Pay).
  • Show back-to-top button: a round button appears after one screen of scrolling; its ring fills as the customer scrolls.
  • Reveal the footer: the page slides up to uncover the footer as customers reach the end. It only runs on large screens when the whole footer fits on screen, and never for customers who have asked their device to reduce motion.

Accessibility

You don't need to do anything: the footer works with a keyboard and screen readers, menus open and close with Enter, the country and language panel closes with Escape, and motion stops for customers who prefer less of it. Keep link text clear (say "Shipping and returns", not "Click here") and give the Image block a meaningful photo; its alt text comes from the image's own alt text in Content → Files.
