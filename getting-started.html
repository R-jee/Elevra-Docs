This guide takes you from installing Elevra to a store that's ready to publish. It takes about 30 minutes.

1. Install and open the editor

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  2. Find Elevra in your theme library and click Customize. The theme editor opens on your home page.

The editor has three parts: the page list at the top, the sections of the current page on the left, and a live preview in the middle. Changes are saved as a draft until you click Save, and customers only see them after you publish the theme.

2. Pick a style

  1. In the editor, click the Theme settings icon (the brush) on the left.
  2. Open Theme styles and choose Elevra, Couture, Onyx, Maison or Aurum.

A style sets fonts, colors, button shapes, spacing and badge looks all at once. It doesn't change your content, so you can try them all. See Theme styles.

3. Add your logo and favicon

  1. Open Theme settings → Logo and favicon and upload your logo under Logo. Any shape works; set its size with Logo width on desktop.
  2. In the same place, upload a Favicon (a small square image shown in browser tabs). Use at least 32 × 32 pixels.

4. Set your colors

Theme settings → Colors has six color schemes. Each scheme is a set of colors that work together: background, text, buttons, borders and sale prices. Sections and the header pick a scheme, so changing a scheme updates every section that uses it.

  • Scheme 1 is the main page background.
  • Scheme 3 is the dark scheme used over photos (slideshows, promo cards, the announcement bar). Keep it dark with light text so text stays readable on images.

When you change colors, the editor doesn't warn you about low contrast. Keep text clearly readable against its background; a contrast checker such as WebAIM's can help.

5. Choose fonts

Theme settings → Typography: pick a heading font and a body font from Shopify's font library. Bold and italic versions load automatically.

6. Build your menus

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Content → Menus.
  2. Edit Main menu for the header. Drag a link under another to create a dropdown; a third level creates a mega menu column.
  3. Edit Footer menu for the footer links.

To add featured products or a promo image to a mega menu, click the Header section in the editor and add a Mega menu promotion or Featured products block.

7. Set up the home page

The home page comes with sections already arranged for your style. Click a section to edit it, drag the ⋮⋮ handle to reorder, the eye icon to hide it, and Add section to add more. See Sections for what each one does.

Use your own photos. Images look best at least 1600 pixels wide. To keep the important part of a photo in view when it's cropped, set a focal point: click the image in the editor, then Edit → choose the focal point.

8. Check your product and collection pages

  1. In the page list at the top of the editor, choose Products → Default product.
  2. The Product information section holds blocks (title, price, variant picker, buy buttons, description and more). Reorder or remove them as you like.
  3. Do the same for Collections → Default collection: choose the Desktop filter layout, Columns on desktop and Columns on mobile, and whether to Show sold-out products last.

9. Cart and delivery settings

In Theme settings → Cart, set Cart type (a drawer that slides in from the side, or a cart page), a Free shipping threshold for the progress message, Products to suggest in the cart, and turn on Show order note, Show discount code field or a Gift wrap product. In Theme settings → Shipping and delivery, set the processing time, fastest and slowest delivery, and the same-day cut-off hour to show estimated delivery dates.

10. Preview, then publish

  1. Use the device switcher at the top of the editor to check mobile and desktop.
  2. Click Preview to browse the store as a customer.
  3. When you're happy, go back to Online Store → Themes and click Publish on Elevra.
