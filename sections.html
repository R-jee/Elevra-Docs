A page is built from sections stacked top to bottom. In the editor, click Add section where you want one, then click it to change its settings. Most sections also have blocks (for example the slides of a slideshow): click Add block inside the section, and drag blocks to reorder them.

Every section has a Color scheme setting (pick one of the six schemes from Theme settings → Colors) and Top padding and Bottom padding to control space around it.

Screenshots show sections on desktop and mobile (for example Product bundle and on mobile).
